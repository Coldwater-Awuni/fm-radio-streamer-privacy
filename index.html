FM Radio Streamer ("the app") plays internet radio stations and, on supported phones, local FM radio. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and who else receives it.

1. The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up. The app does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your favorites, listening history, reminders, listening statistics and recordings are stored only on your device.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are off until you choose to share them, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Premium removes them.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. What the app uses, and why

2.1 Only if you agree: anonymous usage data and crash reports

If you tap Allow when the app asks (or switch it on in Settings → Privacy), the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to receive:

  • anonymous events, such as "a station was played", "the Premium screen was viewed", which language or theme was chosen, and whether the sleep timer or recording was used. These events never contain the names of stations you play, anything you type in search, or your recordings;
  • crash reports and diagnostics: device model, Android version, app version and the technical trace of what failed;
  • an app-installation identifier generated by Firebase. It is not your name, and it is not your advertising ID (the app switches off Firebase's collection of the advertising ID).

Purpose: to find and fix crashes and to understand which features are used, so the app can be improved. If you decline, none of this is sent.

2.2 Advertising (free version)

The free version displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads, prevent fraud and run analytics, the AdMob software in the app automatically collects and uses:

  • your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location);
  • device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and an app-set ID;
  • interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches and taps;
  • diagnostic information about how the app and the ad software perform.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app first shows Google's consent form and only requests ads if you allow it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings. Buying Premium removes ads, and watching an optional rewarded ad turns them off for 24 hours.

2.3 Station directory and playing a station

Station lists, search and station details come from the community directory radio-browser.info, and the audio streams directly from each station's own server. Those servers receive your IP address and ordinary request details, as any website would. When you start a station, the app also tells radio-browser.info which station was played (their popularity counter). No account or name is sent, because none exists.

2.4 Your country

To suggest local stations, the app works out your country on your device from the phone's SIM/network country and language settings. This is not sent anywhere. If you turn on Detect country from IP address (off by default), the app asks ipapi.co for the country of your IP address, so ipapi.co sees your IP address when it does. You can also choose a country yourself.

2.5 Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only remembers on your device that Premium is active, and re-checks this with Google Play.

2.6 What stays on your device

Favorites, recently played stations, reminders, listening statistics, program recordings, and your language, theme and country choices are stored only on your device. Recordings are saved in the app's private storage for your personal listening. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

3. Permissions

  • Internet and network state, Wi-Fi state: to stream radio and to notice when you are offline.
  • Wake lock and foreground service (media playback): to keep audio playing with the screen off.
  • Notifications: for playback controls and the program reminders you create.
  • Boot completed and exact alarms (where allowed): to keep your weekly reminders working after the phone restarts.
  • Vibrate: for reminder notifications.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

  • Consent: anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase), and ad personalisation or ad requests where consent is required (AdMob). You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract: processing of purchases through Google Play, and delivering the radio streams you ask for.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the app secure and working, such as the station directory requests described in 2.3.

6. Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Analytics and crash data held by Google Firebase is kept for the period set in our Firebase project (at most 14 months). AdMob, Google Play, radio-browser.info and ipapi.co keep data under their own policies.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Turn analytics and crash reporting on or off in Settings → Privacy.
  • Reset your advertising ID, and limit ad personalisation, in your Android settings.
  • Clear your history and statistics inside the app, or uninstall it to remove all local data. Step-by-step instructions, including how to ask us to delete analytics and crash data, are on the Delete your data page.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the UK GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data-protection authority.

Because the app has no accounts, we usually cannot tell which analytics data belongs to you. To make a request, email danielcoldwater53@gmail.com and tell us what you would like; we will reply within 30 days and explain what we can and cannot identify.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age your country requires), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Google and the other services above may process data in countries other than your own, using safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where the law requires them.

10. Security

Requests to our service providers (Google, radio-browser.info, ipapi.co) use HTTPS. Radio streams are delivered by the stations themselves and some use plain HTTP; they do not carry personal data from the app, but the station can see your IP address.

11. Content you hear

Stations are run by third parties and are not reviewed by us, so their content and any advertising they play is their own responsibility. Recording is for personal listening only; please respect the rights of broadcasters and artists.

12. Changes to this policy

When the app changes how it handles data, we will update this page and its effective date. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app.
