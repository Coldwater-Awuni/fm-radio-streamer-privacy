Delete your data: FM Radio Streamer

App: FM Radio Streamer (Android, com.lumeteng.fmradiostreamer)
Developer: Daniel Awuni
Contact: danielcoldwater53@gmail.com

FM Radio Streamer has no accounts, so there is no profile on our side to delete. This page explains how to remove the information the app does handle, and what each option deletes or keeps. For the full picture of what is collected, read the Privacy Policy.

1. Delete everything stored on your phone

Your favorites, listening history, reminders, listening statistics, recordings, and your language, theme and country choices are stored only on your device. To delete them:

  • Uninstall the app. All of it is removed.
  • Or keep the app and clear its data: open Android Settings → Apps → FM Radio Streamer → Storage → Clear data.

This takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. We do not hold a copy, so we cannot restore it.

2. Stop anonymous usage data and crash reports

Open the app, go to Settings → Privacy and turn off Share anonymous usage data. Nothing further is sent from that moment. This is also off by default until you choose to allow it.

3. Request deletion of data already sent to analytics

If you allowed anonymous usage statistics or crash reports, they are stored by Google Firebase under a random installation identifier that the app generates, not under your name.

How to ask
1. Send an email to danielcoldwater53@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data: FM Radio Streamer".
2. Say that you want your analytics and crash data deleted, and give your phone model and the approximate dates you used the app. This helps us find it.

What happens

  • We reply within 30 days.
  • We delete the analytics and crash records we can link to your device. Because they are not tied to a name or account, we may not always be able to identify them, and we will tell you if so.
  • Records we do not delete are removed automatically when Firebase's retention period ends, at most 14 months after collection.

4. Advertising data (free version)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which holds its own data about your advertising ID and device. We cannot delete that for you, but you can control it directly:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (the wording varies by phone).
  • Turn off ad personalisation: myadcenter.google.com.

Buying Premium removes ads from the app.

5. Purchases

Payments and purchase records are held by Google Play, not by us, and may be kept as required for tax and accounting. Manage them in the Google Play app under Payments and subscriptions. The app itself stores only a local "Premium is active" flag, which section 1 removes.

6. Station directory

When you play a station, a "station played" counter is sent to the community directory radio-browser.info without your name or any account. We cannot delete from their service, and it is not linked to you.
